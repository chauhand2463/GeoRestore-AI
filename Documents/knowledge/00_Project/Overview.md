# Overview

GeoRestore AI is an AI-based geospatial image restoration system.

## Purpose

Reconstruct degraded or missing information in satellite imagery (cloud
contamination, gaps) while preserving spatial and spectral consistency.

## Scope

- Cloud removal and reconstruction for LISS-IV imagery
- Spectral consistency across bands
- Georeferenced output (GeoTIFF)
